## Description

Water Tally is a simple, private, open-source water tracker for iPhone and iPad. Log a drink in one tap, see your progress toward a daily goal you choose, and keep everything on your device. No ads, no account, and no data collected.

WHO IT'S FOR
• Anyone who wants a quick, calm way to keep track of the water they drink
• People who care about privacy: your log never leaves your device
• Anyone who wants to log water from a Home Screen widget without opening an app
• Apple Health users who want water saved there automatically (optional)

ONE-TAP LOGGING
• Your own buttons for the glasses, cups and bottles you actually use, plus a custom amount
• Log straight from Home Screen widgets, without opening the app
• Undo right after you log; edit or delete entries any time
• Forgot to log yesterday? Go back a day and add it

SEE YOUR DAY AT A GLANCE
• A progress ring toward your daily goal, in ounces or millilitres
• Streaks, and a short note when you reach your goal
• History, calendar, and weekly, monthly, 3-month and yearly trends
• A short weekly summary every Sunday

WIDGETS AND LOCK SCREEN
• Small, medium and large Home Screen widgets with tap-to-log buttons
• Lock Screen widgets to check your progress at a glance

OPTIONAL APPLE HEALTH
• Save each drink to the Health app as Water, kept in step when you edit or delete
• Only Water is read or written, and it's off until you turn it on
• Restore missing entries on a new iPhone

OPTIONAL REMINDERS
• Gentle reminders during the hours you choose
• They stop for the day once you reach your goal

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking
• Works fully offline; the app makes no network requests
• Export your data to CSV or JSON, or delete everything, any time

OPEN SOURCE
The full source code is public under the GNU GPL v3 license, so anyone can see exactly how the app works. Report bugs, suggest features or leave a review from Settings › About & Feedback.

QUICK ANSWERS
• Do I need an account? No. Open it and start logging.
• Are there ads? No.
• Does it work offline? Yes. It never needs the internet.
• Does it work with Apple Health? Yes, if you turn it on.
• Can I use millilitres? Yes, ounces or millilitres everywhere.
• Does it work on iPad? Yes, it's designed for iPhone and iPad.

Water Tally is a simple tracking tool. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. Goals are personal targets you choose. Talk to a healthcare professional about how much fluid is right for you.
