## Description

Water Tally is the simplest way to keep track of the water you drink.

Tap a button, and it's logged. That's it. Water Tally is built to be fast, calm, and private.

ONE-TAP LOGGING
• Big buttons for the glasses and bottles you actually use, plus a custom-amount button
• Undo right after you log, edit or delete entries any time
• Forgot to log yesterday? Swipe back a day and add it

SEE YOUR DAY AT A GLANCE
• A progress ring toward the daily goal you choose
• Streaks and a small celebration when you reach your goal
• History, calendar, and weekly, monthly, and yearly trends

WIDGETS AND LOCK SCREEN
• Home Screen widgets in three sizes, with tap-to-log buttons
• Lock Screen widgets for progress at a glance

OPTIONAL APPLE HEALTH
• Save your water to Apple Health if you want it alongside your other data
• Only Water is accessed, and it's off until you turn it on
• Restore missing entries on a new phone

OPTIONAL REMINDERS
• Gentle reminders during the hours you pick, paused once you reach your goal

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking
• Your entries stay on your device
• Export to CSV or JSON, or delete everything, any time

MADE FOR YOU
• Ounces or millilitres
• Custom button colours and theme
• Designed for iPhone and iPad

Water Tally is a simple tracking tool. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. Goals are personal targets you choose. Talk to a healthcare professional about how much fluid is right for you.

## App Review notes
Water Tally is a simple water log. No login is required.
- Apple Health is optional: Settings > "Save to Apple Health". The app only reads/writes the Water type (dietary water) and only reads when the user taps "Restore from Apple Health".
- Reminders are optional local notifications (Settings > Drink reminders).
- First launch shows a short setup flow ending in a required acknowledgment of the Terms, Privacy Policy and Medical Disclaimer.
- Widgets: long-press the Home Screen > + > Water Tally.
- The app makes no network requests.
