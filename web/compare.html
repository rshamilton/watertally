What Water Tally doesn't do (yet)

To be fair about it: other apps may suit you better if you want an Apple Watch app, caffeine or nutrition tracking, a cartoon character or plant that grows as you drink, or syncing across devices through an account. Water Tally keeps to simple, private water logging on iPhone and iPad.

Why Water Tally is different

  • No ads and nothing to buy. There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions.
  • No account. Open it and start logging.
  • No data collected. Entries stay on your device and the app makes no network requests.
  • Open source. The code is public on GitHub (GPL v3), so anyone can check these claims.
  • Log without opening the app. Home Screen widgets have tap-to-log buttons.
