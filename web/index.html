Water Tally

Simple, private water tracking for iPhone. Log a drink in one tap, see your day at a glance, and keep your data on your device.

Download on the App Store

Free. iPhone and iPad, iOS 17 or later.

  • One-tap logging

    Big, friendly buttons for the glasses and bottles you actually use, plus a custom-amount button for everything else. Made a mistake? Undo it right away.

  • Widgets & Lock Screen

    Check your progress and log a drink from the Home Screen or Lock Screen without opening the app.

  • Trends & streaks

    A calendar, daily history, weekly and monthly charts, and streaks to keep you going.

  • Optional Apple Health

    Save your water to Apple Health if you want it alongside your other data. It is off until you turn it on.

  • Gentle reminders

    Optional reminders you schedule yourself. They are created on your device and pause once you reach your goal.

  • Private by design

    No account. No ads. No analytics. No tracking. Your entries stay on your device, and you can export or delete them any time.

Please note

Water Tally is a simple logging tool. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. Goals are personal targets you choose. Talk to a qualified healthcare professional about how much fluid is right for you. Read the full medical disclaimer.
